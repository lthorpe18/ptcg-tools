Tournament decision

Cut / ID Calculator

Deterministic final-round ID check. No hidden tie-rate or simulation assumptions.

An ID adds one point to both players. Without complete pairings, every supplied player is conservatively allowed to gain three points. With complete pairing data, the bound respects the fact that paired players cannot both win. “Depends on tiebreaks” means points alone cannot lock the cut.
Standalone organiser utility. Tournaments created here stay inside Tournament Manager and do not create Compete events, Tournament Day records, Season results or account participation records.
Card maths

Odds

Exact hypergeometric probabilities for common Pokémon TCG questions.

These are exact combinatorial probabilities. They assume the relevant cards are uniformly distributed in the remaining deck and do not model search effects, sequencing or known-card information unless you adjust the inputs.
